TrustGraph

Recommendation Stability Tool

Fires paraphrased, brand-neutral queries at several production LLMs and records which vendor each model actually recommends. Reports a Trust Score (Eq. 3), a Recommendation Stability Index (Eq. 5), and a head-to-head win rate — per model and combined. The hero is the grid, not the average: an aggregate percentage hides the phrasings where a recommendation is fragile.

Each cell is one model's answer to one phrasing, classified into the four states below. Cells are buttons — activate one to read the response text behind it. A red band confined to one framing (say, every "cheapest" phrasing) is the finding; an averaged score cannot show it.

Four states, not two. Collapsing Both into Win, or Neither into Loss, would discard the distinction between winning outright and merely appearing alongside a competitor — which is the vanity-metric collapse this instrument exists to avoid. A corner tick marks a cell where a tracked name was present but not recommended.

Methodology & measurement boundary

Trust score
Trust(e,c,m) = responses recommending e / total paraphrases — Eq. (3). A binary indicator per response; "recommended" excludes names that appear only as a caveat, a counterexample, or a rejected alternative.
RSI
RSI = 1 − Var/0.25 — Eq. (5), where 0.25 is the maximum variance of a {0,1} indicator. Note the direction: RSI measures consistency, so an entity recommended in 0% of phrasings is as "stable" as one recommended in 100%. Each badge therefore states its direction rather than showing the number alone.
AITC
§3.5 writes AITC as the weighted sum Σ w·Trust. Taken literally that is unbounded and not comparable to Trust, so this implementation normalises by Σ w — a weighted mean on [0,1], in the same units as Trust. Weights are a stated market-share prior, not a measurement.
Extraction
Two independent passes. A boundary-anchored matcher establishes presence; a structured LLM call judges recommendation. The matcher is authoritative on presence, the model on recommendation, and any model-only mention must be backed by a verbatim span that genuinely occurs in the response — otherwise a hallucinated mention would fabricate a Win. Agreement between passes is reported, not hidden.
Temperature
Sampling temperature is pinned to 0 wherever the provider allows it. RSI is defined as variance across phrasings; leaving sampling free would mix in sampling noise and change what the number means.
Not measured
Why a model recommends something; training-data attribution; pretrain / fine-tune / RLHF contribution; any ground-truth internal "trust" representation — none exists. This measures the behavioural shadow of a latent structure, not the structure itself (Table 1).
Deliberately skipped
No source-corroboration graph, no HITS iteration, no web-authority seeding, no longitudinal storage. Those stay in the paper as the theory explaining why a cross-model, paraphrase-averaged score is the right thing to measure — they are not on this artefact's critical path.
